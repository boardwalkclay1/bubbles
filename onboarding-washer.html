💡 Tips for More Jobs

  • Offer specialty services like delicates or express (same-day) — charge a premium for these.
  • Respond to requests quickly — clients often accept whoever responds first.
  • Keep your service area focused so travel time doesn't eat into your earnings.
  • Stay available during peak times: Monday mornings and Sunday evenings are busy.
  • Ask clients for a review after delivery — social proof brings repeat business.
  • Offer package deals (3 loads for $X) to lock in repeat customers.

🌟 Best Practices for Quality Service

  • Sort clothes carefully — whites, darks, and colors separately.
  • Always check pockets before washing.
  • Use the correct wash temperature per item type.
  • Fold or hang clothes promptly after drying to prevent wrinkles.
  • Bag items neatly — presentation matters and leads to better tips.
  • Communicate proactively with clients about pickup/delivery times.

🛡️ Safety & Verification Tips

  • Meet clients in well-lit, public areas or building lobbies for first pickups.
  • Document items received — a quick photo protects both parties.
  • Never accept jobs that require entering a private residence alone.
  • Trust your gut — it's okay to decline a job that feels off.
  • Keep a record of all transactions for tax purposes.
