🧺 How to Use Laundry Bubbles

Everything you need to know to get your laundry done hassle-free.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email and select "Client" as your role. It only takes 30 seconds.

2

Unlock access for $1.50

Pay a small $1.50 fee via Stripe for 24 hours of full platform access. Cancel anytime — no recurring charges unless you log in again.

3

Post a pickup request

Enter your pickup address, describe what you need washed (delicates, regular, dry-clean), and optionally set a budget.

4

A washer accepts your job

Local washers in your area will see your request. Once accepted, you'll see the washer's name appear on your order.

5

Track on the live map

The live map on your dashboard shows nearby washers. Your washer will update their location as they come to pick up.

6

Pay your washer directly

Agree on payment with your washer via Apple Pay, Cash App, PayPal, or card. The app just connects you — payments are peer-to-peer.

💡 Tips for a great experience

  • Be specific about delicates — mention brand names or fabrics that need special care.
  • Add detailed pickup instructions (gate codes, apartment numbers, best time windows).
  • Set a fair budget — washers can see it and are more likely to accept reasonable offers.
  • Check your order status page to track progress through picked_up → in_progress → delivered.
  • Communicate directly with your washer using the phone number they've shared in their profile.

💳 Payment Explained

The $1.50/24hr fee gives you access to the Laundry Bubbles network. This is separate from what you pay your washer. Think of it like a marketplace fee for connecting with local service providers. Your 24-hour window starts when payment is confirmed.

🛡️ Safety Guidelines

  • Only share your address and phone number with confirmed washers (ones who have accepted your job).
  • For first-time pickups, consider meeting at a neutral location like your building lobby.
  • Document any special items before handing them over.
  • Rate your washer after delivery to help build community trust.
